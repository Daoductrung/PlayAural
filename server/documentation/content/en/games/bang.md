\*\*BANG! The Bullet\*\*

\*\*The story\*\*

The sun hangs over a lawless frontier town. The Sheriff stands in public, the Deputies hide among the crowd, the Outlaws wait for a clean shot, and a lone Renegade plans to outlive them all. Every friendly drink, missed bullet, and card slipped from a sleeve may reveal who is on whose side.

Designed by Emiliano Sciarra in 2002, the original BANG! is a hidden-role card game inspired by Spaghetti Westerns. PlayAural turns that showdown into an audio-first game for 3 to 8 players and handles the deck, secret information, distance, draw checks, events, damage, and victory automatically.

\*\*Your objective\*\*

Your secret role tells you whom you must protect or eliminate. At tables of 4 to 8, only the Sheriff begins revealed; every other role stays private until that player is eliminated.

\* \*\*Sheriff:\*\* Eliminate every Outlaw and Renegade.
\* \*\*Deputy:\*\* Keep the Sheriff alive and help eliminate every Outlaw and Renegade.
\* \*\*Outlaw:\*\* Eliminate the Sheriff. Outlaws win together even if one has already been eliminated.
\* \*\*Renegade:\*\* Be the last surviving player after every other player, including the Sheriff, has been eliminated.

Role counts are: Sheriff, Renegade, and two Outlaws at 4 players; add one Deputy at 5; add a third Outlaw at 6; add a second Deputy at 7; at 8 players use one Sheriff, two Deputies, three Outlaws, and two Renegades.

Landing the killing blow on the Sheriff does not by itself make a Renegade win. The Renegade wins only when the Sheriff is eliminated and that Renegade is the sole survivor. If anyone else is still in play when the Sheriff falls, the Outlaws win, even if every Outlaw was eliminated earlier.

At 3 players, the Deputy, Outlaw, and Renegade are all public. The Deputy's assigned target is the Renegade, the Renegade's is the Outlaw, and the Outlaw's is the Deputy. Personally eliminate your assigned target to win immediately. If you personally eliminate the wrong opponent, draw three cards and the final two players continue until only one survives. The Deputy takes the first turn.

\*\*What you need to know first\*\*

Life is your health. If damage leaves you at zero or less and you cannot recover immediately, you are eliminated. Cards in your hand are private. Your character, current life, hand count, weapon, and face-up cards are public. At 4 to 8 players, your role is private unless you are the Sheriff or have been eliminated.

Most turns are simple: resolve anything waiting at the start of your turn, draw cards, play as many legal cards or abilities as you want, then choose End turn. You will usually attack with BANG!, defend with Missed!, improve your range with a Weapon, and manage your hand so it does not exceed your life.

In this manual, a player “currently in play” means a normal active player or a Ghost Town ghost during that ghost's turn. An eliminated player is otherwise out of play. PlayAural applies this distinction automatically when counting targets, cards, and effects.

\*\*Before the first turn\*\*

Each player receives a public main character, a public alternate character, and a role. Your main character sets your ability and maximum life. The Sheriff gains one maximum life. Your opening hand contains one card for each point of starting life.

At 4 to 8 players, the Sheriff takes the first turn. At 3 players, the Deputy starts. The opening announcement privately tells you your role, character, life, and objective, then identifies the roles that are public. Use Read your hand and in-play cards whenever you want to review your own cards.

The alternate character has no ability unless New Identity appears. Anyone without an equipped Weapon uses the permanent Colt .45 with range 1. The host chooses whether to include expanded cards and characters and which event set to use; both options are explained later in this manual.

\*\*A turn, step by step\*\*

1. Listen to the Next instruction at the top of the menu. PlayAural first resolves the current event, Dynamite, Jail, and any character or event choices in the order they must occur.
2. Complete the draw step. Normally you draw two cards, but a character or event may replace or alter that draw.
3. Choose a card or an available character ability. PlayAural shows only legal responses, costs, and targets. You may continue playing while the menu says it is your play step.
4. Choose End turn. If your hand has more cards than your current life, select exactly the stated number of cards and confirm the discard. Sean Mallory instead has a hand limit of 10.
5. Play passes to the next active player. Your own cards remain visible but unavailable in the menu while someone else acts, so reviewing them cannot change the game. Press H for a concise private reading of both your hand and your own face-up cards.

If an effect asks someone to respond or choose, the current action pauses. No player may begin an unrelated action until that response or choice is finished. Trying an unavailable action tells you who is deciding and the specific step still pending without revealing private cards. Check whose turn also reports this pending player and required action, even when that player is responding outside their own turn.

\*\*Cards, suits, and draw checks\*\*

Cards have a suit and rank. Clubs and Spades are black; Diamonds and Hearts are red. Most cards ignore these marks, but draw checks, character abilities, events, and Handcuffs use them. Ranks run 2 through 10, Jack, Queen, King, and Ace.

A draw check reveals and discards the top card. Hearts usually succeeds for Barrel, Jail, and Vendetta. Dynamite explodes only on Spades from 2 through 9. Lucky Duke reveals two cards and chooses which one counts. Blessing treats every card as Hearts; Curse treats every card as Spades.

\* \*\*Brown cards:\*\* Resolve immediately, then go to the discard pile.
\* \*\*Blue cards:\*\* Remain face up in play. You may not have two non-Weapon cards with the same name in front of you.
\* \*\*Green cards:\*\* Are placed face up without choosing a target. They cannot be used during the turn in which they were placed. Starting on a later turn, discard one to use its effect and choose a target only then. A stolen green card returns to hand and must be placed and wait again.

\*\*Menus, targeting, and cancellation\*\*

Choose a card to play. If it has one extra-card cost, choose that card first; no card is consumed until the complete play succeeds. Tequila, for example, asks for one extra hand card and then its recipient. Cancel at either step to keep both cards.

Choosing a target commits the play immediately. Single-target menus therefore contain legal targets followed by Cancel and never add a redundant Confirm control. If exactly one legal target or response exists, PlayAural may resolve that forced choice automatically. Confirm appears only for genuine selections of two or more cards, such as excess discards or Sid Ketchum's ability.

For Cat Balou, Panic!, Brawl, Rag Time, Can Can, and Conestoga, a visible card can be chosen directly. A hand is secret, so choosing the hand takes one random card. Placing a targeted green card such as Can Can or Knife never asks for a target; using it on a later turn does.

\*\*Distance and weapons\*\*

Base distance is the shorter number of occupied seats around the table; eliminated players do not count. A distance can never fall below 1. Scope, Binocular, and Rose Doolan reduce how far you see other players. Mustang, Hideout, and Paul Regret increase how far others see their owner. Ambush sets every base distance to 1 before modifiers.

Your weapon range limits BANG!, Pepperbox, and effects that explicitly use current weapon range. Panic!, Punch, Derringer, and Knife require distance 1. Springfield and Buffalo Rifle ignore distance.

Only one Weapon card can be equipped. Equipping one never fires: it covers the Colt .45, discards the previously equipped Weapon card, and publicly announces the swap. Losing or disabling the equipped card immediately makes the Colt .45 active again.

\* \*\*Schofield:\*\* Revolver, range 2.
\* \*\*Remington:\*\* Rifle, range 3.
\* \*\*Rev. Carabine:\*\* Carbine rifle, range 4.
\* \*\*Winchester:\*\* Lever-action rifle, range 5.
\* \*\*Volcanic:\*\* Repeating pistol, range 1; removes the normal one-BANG!-card limit during your turn.

\*\*Attacks, defense, recovery, and elimination\*\*

Any attack showing the BANG! symbol asks its target for one Missed! effect unless the card says otherwise. This includes attacks such as BANG!, Punch, and Knife. Missed!, Dodge, a successful Barrel, and ready defensive green cards can supply that effect. Dodge then draws one card. Slab the Killer's BANG! cards require two Missed! effects, but his ability does not strengthen Punch, Knife, Gatling, Howitzer, Duel, or other cards that are not BANG! cards.

Resolve every available Barrel check before choosing a card from hand. Each failed check leaves the attack pending; it does not force you to use Missed! until all remaining Barrel checks have been used or skipped. Jourdonnais with a real Barrel therefore has two separate checks. Each successful check supplies one Missed! effect.

If the target supplies too few Missed! effects, the target loses one life. Duel instead alternates BANG! responses, and the first player unable or unwilling to answer loses one life. Indians! asks every other player for BANG! rather than Missed!.

At zero or less life, play enough Beer from hand or use Sid Ketchum's ability enough times to return above zero. Beer may be played at full life but is discarded without healing. Beer also has no healing effect when only two players remain. Saloon and Tequila still work with two players. Life never exceeds maximum life.

An eliminated player reveals their role and discards all cards unless Vulture Sam collects them. If both Vulture Sam and Vera Custer copying Vulture Sam are active, they split the cards: the collector nearest clockwise from the eliminated player chooses first, then the two collectors alternate until no cards remain. When cards are discarded normally, the eliminated player chooses their exact order across both the hand and cards in play, one card at a time; Finish uses the remaining menu order.

The player responsible for eliminating an Outlaw draws three cards. If the Sheriff eliminates a Deputy, the Sheriff discards the entire hand and every card in play; a Sheriff who is also Vulture Sam collects first, then pays the penalty. Damage caused by a player's own Duel does not give the opponent an elimination reward.

\*\*Special attack rules\*\*

During Duel, distance, Barrel, and Missed! do not apply. The challenged player answers first, and the two players alternate discarding BANG! cards. The first player who cannot or chooses not to answer loses one life.

During Indians!, every other player responds in clockwise order. Each must discard one BANG! card or lose one life. During Gatling and Howitzer, each other player instead needs one Missed! effect. PlayAural announces each person's result before moving to the next response.

\*\*Complete card reference\*\*

\*\*Immediate attacks and defenses\*\*

\* \*\*BANG!:\*\* Fire at one player within current weapon range. Normally only one BANG! card per turn.
\* \*\*Missed!:\*\* Supply one Missed! effect against an attack showing the BANG! symbol.
\* \*\*Dodge:\*\* Supply one Missed! effect against an attack showing the BANG! symbol, then draw one card.
\* \*\*Punch:\*\* Attack one player at distance 1; one Missed! effect prevents the damage.
\* \*\*Springfield:\*\* Discard one extra hand card to shoot any other player at any distance.
\* \*\*Duel:\*\* Challenge any other player. The target answers first, and the two players alternate BANG! cards until one stops and loses one life.
\* \*\*Gatling:\*\* Shoot every other player; each needs one Missed! effect.
\* \*\*Indians!:\*\* Every other player discards one BANG! card or loses one life.

\*\*Drawing, healing, and table effects\*\*

\* \*\*Beer:\*\* Regain one life, including during lethal recovery. It may be discarded at full life or with two players left, but then heals nothing.
\* \*\*Saloon:\*\* Every player currently in play regains one life. The Reverend does not block Saloon.
\* \*\*Tequila:\*\* Discard one extra hand card; choose any player currently in play, including yourself, to regain one life.
\* \*\*Whisky:\*\* Discard one extra hand card to regain two life.
\* \*\*Stagecoach:\*\* Draw two cards.
\* \*\*Wells Fargo:\*\* Draw three cards.
\* \*\*General Store:\*\* Reveal one card per player currently in play. Starting with the actor and moving clockwise, each player chooses one. The revealed offer and every player's pick are public.

\*\*Taking and discarding cards\*\*

\* \*\*Panic!:\*\* At distance 1, take one chosen card in play or one random hand card.
\* \*\*Cat Balou:\*\* At any distance, make another player lose one chosen card in play or one random hand card.
\* \*\*Brawl:\*\* Discard one extra hand card, then make every other player lose one chosen card in play or one random hand card.
\* \*\*Rag Time:\*\* Discard one extra hand card, then take one chosen card in play or one random hand card from any other player.

\*\*Blue cards in play\*\*

\* \*\*Barrel:\*\* When an attack showing the BANG! symbol targets you, draw-check for Hearts to gain one Missed! effect. Use or skip all Barrel checks before choosing a defense card.
\* \*\*Scope and Binocular:\*\* Each reduces the distance at which you see every other player by 1.
\* \*\*Mustang and Hideout:\*\* Each increases the distance at which every other player sees you by 1.
\* \*\*Jail:\*\* Place on another legal player. At that player's turn start, Hearts removes it safely; another suit skips the turn. The Sheriff cannot be jailed except at a 3-player table.
\* \*\*Dynamite:\*\* At its owner's turn start, Spades 2 through 9 makes it explode for three life. Otherwise it passes to the next clockwise player who does not already have Dynamite. It is discarded before elimination rewards are collected.
\* \*\*Schofield, Remington, Rev. Carabine, Winchester, and Volcanic:\*\* Weapon cards described in Distance and weapons above.

\*\*Green delayed-use cards\*\*

\* \*\*Bible:\*\* Discard as one Missed! effect, then draw one card. If the same attack still needs another Missed! effect, an eligible card just drawn by Bible may be used immediately.
\* \*\*Iron Plate, Sombrero, and Ten Gallon Hat:\*\* Discard as one Missed! effect.
\* \*\*Buffalo Rifle:\*\* Choose and shoot any other player, regardless of distance.
\* \*\*Can Can:\*\* Choose another player to lose one chosen card in play or one random hand card.
\* \*\*Canteen:\*\* Regain one life.
\* \*\*Conestoga:\*\* Choose another player and take one chosen card in play or one random hand card.
\* \*\*Derringer:\*\* Shoot a player at distance 1; after the shot resolves, draw one card.
\* \*\*Howitzer:\*\* Shoot every other player; each needs one Missed! effect.
\* \*\*Knife:\*\* Stab a player at distance 1; one Missed! effect prevents the damage.
\* \*\*Pepperbox:\*\* Shoot a player within current weapon range.
\* \*\*Pony Express:\*\* Draw three cards.

\*\*Complete character reference\*\*

Life shown below is the character's normal maximum before the Sheriff's bonus.

\* \*\*Bart Cassidy — 4 life:\*\* After damage is survived, draw one card per life lost. If the last life is lost, Beer already in hand must save Bart before this draw occurs.
\* \*\*Black Jack — 4 life:\*\* Reveal the second normal draw; on Hearts or Diamonds, draw one extra card.
\* \*\*Calamity Janet — 4 life:\*\* Use BANG! as Missed! and Missed! as BANG!. A converted Missed! obeys the normal BANG! limit.
\* \*\*El Gringo — 3 life:\*\* After surviving damage caused by another player's card, take one random hand card from that player per life lost. Self-played Duel and Dynamite do not trigger this.
\* \*\*Jesse Jones — 4 life:\*\* The first normal draw may be taken randomly from another player's hand.
\* \*\*Jourdonnais — 4 life:\*\* Always has a virtual Barrel, which stacks with one real Barrel. Both checks come before cards such as Missed! or Dodge.
\* \*\*Kit Carlson — 4 life:\*\* Look at the top three cards, keep the required draw count, and return the rest to the top without changing their relative order.
\* \*\*Lucky Duke — 4 life:\*\* Reveal two cards for every draw check and choose which result counts.
\* \*\*Paul Regret — 3 life:\*\* Every other player sees Paul at distance +1.
\* \*\*Pedro Ramirez — 4 life:\*\* The first normal draw may be the face-up top card of the discard pile.
\* \*\*Rose Doolan — 4 life:\*\* Sees every other player at distance -1, to a minimum of 1.
\* \*\*Sid Ketchum — 4 life:\*\* Between completed card effects, even outside his turn, discard exactly two hand cards to regain one life. He may also do this during his own lethal recovery, but cannot interrupt an unresolved card or choice.
\* \*\*Slab the Killer — 4 life:\*\* Each BANG! card requires two Missed! effects.
\* \*\*Suzy Lafayette — 4 life:\*\* When her hand becomes empty, draw one card after the current effect finishes.
\* \*\*Vulture Sam — 4 life:\*\* Collect every hand card and card in play belonging to an eliminated player. If Vera Custer is copying this ability, the two collectors alternate choices, beginning with the one nearest clockwise from the eliminated player.
\* \*\*Willy the Kid — 4 life:\*\* May play any number of BANG! cards during his turn.
\* \*\*Apache Kid — 3 life:\*\* Cards of Diamonds played by other players do not affect him, except during Duel.
\* \*\*Belle Star — 4 life:\*\* During her turn, cards in front of other players have no effect.
\* \*\*Bill Noface — 4 life:\*\* Draw one card plus one per missing life during the normal draw phase.
\* \*\*Chuck Wengam — 4 life:\*\* During his turn, lose one non-final life to draw two cards; repeat while legal.
\* \*\*Doc Holyday — 4 life:\*\* Once per turn, discard two hand cards for one BANG! effect within weapon range.
\* \*\*Elena Fuente — 3 life:\*\* Use any hand card as Missed!.
\* \*\*Greg Digger — 4 life:\*\* Regain two life whenever another player is eliminated.
\* \*\*Herb Hunter — 4 life:\*\* Draw two cards whenever another player is eliminated.
\* \*\*José Delgado — 4 life:\*\* Up to twice per turn, discard a blue hand card to draw two cards.
\* \*\*Molly Stark — 4 life:\*\* Outside her turn, draw a replacement after playing or voluntarily discarding BANG!, Missed!, or Beer from hand. Dodge and cards already in play do not qualify; Duel replacements wait until Duel ends.
\* \*\*Pat Brennan — 4 life:\*\* Instead of normal drawing, take exactly one card in play from any player. Train Arrival then adds its extra draw, while Abandoned Mine and Peyote replace this choice.
\* \*\*Pixie Pete — 3 life:\*\* Draw three cards in the normal draw phase.
\* \*\*Sean Mallory — 3 life:\*\* Has an end-of-turn hand limit of 10 instead of current life.
\* \*\*Tequila Joe — 4 life:\*\* Each Beer he plays restores two life instead of one.
\* \*\*Vera Custer — 3 life:\*\* At turn start, copy another living character's ability until her next turn.
\* \*\*Claus "The Saint" — 3 life:\*\* Draw one more card than the number of players currently in play, give one to every other player currently in play, and keep two.
\* \*\*Johnny Kisch — 4 life:\*\* When he puts a card in play, discard every other card in play with the same name.
\* \*\*Uncle Will — 4 life:\*\* Once per turn, use any hand card as General Store.

\*\*Turn-changing event rules\*\*

When events are enabled, the first event is revealed at the start of the Sheriff's second turn, then a new one at each later Sheriff turn. At 3 players, the Deputy is the event anchor. A new event replaces the old one. The last event remains active for the rest of the game. Mixed rules use 12 random nonfinal events and one random finale.

\*\*High Noon event family\*\*

\* \*\*Blessing:\*\* Every card's suit is Hearts.
\* \*\*Curse:\*\* Every card's suit is Spades.
\* \*\*Ghost Town:\*\* Eliminated players return for their turns as ghosts, draw three cards, cannot be eliminated during that turn, and leave again at turn end. A ghost may regain and spend life during the turn but still leaves at its end.
\* \*\*Gold Rush:\*\* Ordinary turns run counterclockwise. General Store, Dynamite, and effects explicitly saying clockwise stay clockwise.
\* \*\*Hangover:\*\* Character abilities do not work. The Sheriff's extra maximum life remains.
\* \*\*Shootout:\*\* Each player may play a second BANG! card during their turn.
\* \*\*The Daltons:\*\* On reveal, each player with at least one blue card in play chooses and discards one blue card.
\* \*\*The Doctor:\*\* On reveal, every living player tied for the lowest life regains one life.
\* \*\*The Reverend:\*\* Beer cards cannot be played. Saloon and other healing still work.
\* \*\*The Sermon:\*\* The active player cannot use BANG! cards during the turn, including a Missed! converted by Calamity Janet and BANG! responses in Duel.
\* \*\*Thirst:\*\* Draw one fewer card in the normal draw phase.
\* \*\*Train Arrival:\*\* Draw one additional card in the normal draw phase.
\* \*\*Handcuffs:\*\* After drawing, choose the only suit you may play from hand during your turn. This includes your own BANG! answers in Duel and Beer used for lethal recovery during that turn. Other players responding to your actions are not restricted by your declaration.
\* \*\*New Identity:\*\* At turn start, optionally replace the main character with the public alternate character, adopt that character's maximum life, and set current life to 2. The change is permanent.
\* \*\*High Noon:\*\* Permanent finale. Lose one life at the start of every turn.

\*\*Fistful event family\*\*

\* \*\*Abandoned Mine:\*\* During phase one, take the face-up cards from the top of the discard pile until it is empty, then finish the required draw from the draw pile. At phase three, put excess hand cards face down on top of the draw pile, even when the discard pile started empty or ran out.
\* \*\*Ambush:\*\* Base distance between every pair of players is 1; modifiers still apply.
\* \*\*Blood Brothers:\*\* At turn start, optionally lose one non-final life so another injured living player regains one. No prompt appears if nobody can be healed.
\* \*\*Dead Man:\*\* The first eliminated player returns once, at that player's next turn, with two life and two cards, then performs the normal draw step.
\* \*\*Hard Liquor:\*\* Instead of normal drawing, regain one life. At full life the skipped draw heals nothing.
\* \*\*Lasso:\*\* Cards in play have no effect, but remain in place and may still be targeted. Jail and Dynamite stay with their owners and do not perform their turn-start checks until Lasso is replaced.
\* \*\*Law of the West:\*\* Reveal the second normal draw and play it during the play phase if it still has a legal play. It cannot be spent as another action's extra cost.
\* \*\*Peyote:\*\* Replace normal drawing with repeated red-or-black guesses. Reveal every guessed card to the whole table. Keep each correctly guessed card; the first wrong guess is discarded and ends the draw.
\* \*\*Ranch:\*\* Once after drawing, discard any number of hand cards and draw the same number.
\* \*\*Ricochet:\*\* Discard BANG! cards to attack cards in play at any distance. The owner may use one Missed! effect—including a successful Barrel or Jourdonnais check—to save each attacked card.
\* \*\*Russian Roulette:\*\* Starting with the Sheriff, or Deputy at 3 players, players move clockwise and each supply one Missed! effect. If everyone succeeds, the roulette continues around the table again; the first failure loses two life and ends it. The target order is fixed by seating, not randomly selected.
\* \*\*Sniper:\*\* Discard two BANG! cards to shoot one player within weapon range; the target needs two Missed! effects.
\* \*\*The Judge:\*\* No player may put a blue or green card in play.
\* \*\*Vendetta:\*\* At turn end, Hearts on a draw check grants one extra turn. The extra turn cannot trigger Vendetta again.
\* \*\*A Fistful of Cards:\*\* Permanent finale. At turn start, face one avoidable BANG! for every card currently in hand.

\*\*Options\*\*

The two lobby options are independent: changing Expanded cards and characters never turns Turn-changing events on or off.

\* \*\*Expanded cards and characters:\*\* On by default.
  \* \*\*On:\*\* Use all 120 play cards and all 34 characters: 16 standard characters, 15 additional characters, and 3 bonus characters. The extra 40 play cards include green delayed-use cards and additional attacks, defenses, healing, drawing, stealing, and equipment. Tables may have 3 to 8 players.
  \* \*\*Off:\*\* Use the 80-card standard play deck and 19 characters: the 16 standard characters plus the 3 bonus characters. Green delayed-use cards are absent. Tables must have 4 to 7 players; PlayAural blocks Start at 3 or 8 and explains how to fix the setup.

\* \*\*Turn-changing events:\*\* Mixed set is the default. Events alter one rule at a time. The first event appears at the start of the Sheriff's second turn, or the Deputy's second turn at a 3-player table. Each later anchor turn replaces it. The finale never gets replaced.
  \* \*\*Off — standard turns:\*\* No event deck; the rules in the event references above do not activate.
  \* \*\*High Noon set:\*\* Shuffle its 14 ordinary events, then finish with the lasting High Noon finale, which deals one life at the start of every turn.
  \* \*\*A Fistful of Cards set:\*\* Shuffle its 14 ordinary events, then finish with the lasting A Fistful of Cards finale, which launches one avoidable shot per card in the active player's hand.
  \* \*\*Mixed set:\*\* Randomly choose 12 ordinary events from both families, then add either finale at random.

Use Read current event or press E whenever an event changes; PlayAural reads the exact active rule.

\*\*Information and accessible play\*\*

Read your hand and in-play cards privately announces your hand first, then your own face-up cards. These short lists contain names, ranks, and suits but no rules descriptions. The action remains available outside your turn, including while another player is choosing. Full card descriptions stay in the card action rows where you decide what to play or use.

Read distances opens a live status panel. Its first row shows your current Weapon and range; the other stable rows show your distance to each player still in play. Read the table opens a separate live public panel with the active turn, current event, life, hand counts, characters, revealed roles, weapons, and only the names of cards in play. Both panels update while open without forcing you to remember a long spoken list.

Read life reports your current and maximum life. Read role and character reports your role, ability, alternate character, life, and Weapon. Read card piles reports the draw-pile size, discard-pile size, and top discard. Read current event explains the active event. Check whose turn reports both the active turn and any pending out-of-turn choice. Who's at the table opens the current table roster.

Time-critical responses appear in the current menu on touch clients. Speech announces every rule-relevant result, while distinct sounds reinforce attacks, defenses, damage, healing, elimination, and victory without replacing the spoken information.

\*\*Results\*\*

BANG! is scoreless. The game announces the winning side and every member of that winning role team, including eliminated team members when the role rules award them the win. It does not expose score or leaderboard actions.
