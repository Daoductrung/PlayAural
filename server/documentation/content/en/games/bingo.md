\*\*Bingo\*\*

Bingo is a classic number-calling game for 2 to 12 players. Each player gets an independently-shuffled 5x5 card with columns B-I-N-G-O and a free center space. The game calls one number at a time from the full 1 to 75 pool, and players mark their card as numbers are called. The first player to complete the chosen pattern and claim it wins.

\*\*Gameplay\*\*

At the start of the round, everyone receives a card. From then on, a new number is called automatically at a fixed interval:

\* \*\*Navigate your card:\*\* Left and right arrows move between the five letters, B-I-N-G-O; up and down arrows move between the five numbers within a column. Every square you land on announces its full identity directly, such as "B 2" or "O 75".

\* \*\*Mark a square:\*\* Press Enter on a square to mark it, and again to unmark it. You can mark any square at any time, whether or not its number has actually been called — marking ahead isn't blocked. The free center square starts marked and stays that way for the whole round: it counts as marked for any pattern that includes it (Letter X, Blackout), and Enter does nothing to it, since there's nothing to mark or unmark.

\* \*\*Claim Bingo:\*\* Press B once your marked squares complete the winning pattern. The claim is checked at this point: if every marked square in the completed pattern was genuinely called, you win. If one of those squares was marked before its number came up, the claim is rejected and names that specific square, so you know it just wasn't its time yet.

Completing the pattern is not itself a win -- only claiming it (with B) is. If all 75 numbers get called and nobody has claimed by the time the usual post-call window runs out, the round simply ends with no winner, even if someone's card already qualifies.

\*\*Winning Patterns\*\*

The host chooses one pattern before the round starts:

\* \*\*Any Line:\*\* Complete any single row, column, or diagonal. The middle row, the middle column, and both diagonals all pass through the free center square, so those four need one fewer real mark than the rest.

\* \*\*Four Corners:\*\* Mark all four corner squares of the card.

\* \*\*Letter X:\*\* Mark both diagonals, forming an X across the card. The free center square already counts as marked, so only the rest of each diagonal is left.

\* \*\*Blackout:\*\* Mark every square on the card. The free center square already counts as marked, so only the rest is left.

\*\*Customizable Options\*\*

\* \*\*Winning Pattern:\*\* Any Line, Four Corners, Letter X, or Blackout (default Any Line).

\* \*\*Call Interval:\*\* 5, 15, 30, 45, or 60 seconds between each called number (default 15).

\*\*Checking Called Numbers\*\*

\*\*Check Called Numbers\*\* opens a live panel showing how many of the 75 numbers have been called so far, along with the 10 most recently called numbers.

\*\*Keyboard Shortcuts\*\*

\* \*\*Left/Right:\*\* Move between the B-I-N-G-O letters.

\* \*\*Up/Down:\*\* Move between the numbers within a column.

\* \*\*Enter:\*\* Mark or unmark the selected square.

\* \*\*B:\*\* Claim Bingo.

\* \*\*R:\*\* Repeat the last number called.

\* \*\*C:\*\* Check called numbers.
