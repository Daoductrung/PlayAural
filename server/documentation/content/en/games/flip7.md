**Flip 7**

Flip 7 is a push-your-luck card game for 2 to 10 players. Each round, players collect number cards and decide whether to draw again or stop and bank their points. The first player to reach the target score after a completed round wins.

**Goal**

Score points by keeping number cards in your area. You can stop to bank your current round total, but drawing a number you already have makes you bust and score nothing for that round.

**Gameplay**

At the start of each round, every player still in the game receives one card. Then players take turns choosing:

* **Draw a card:** Turn over one more card and resolve it.
* **Stop and bank:** End your round safely and bank your current points when the round ends.

Your area is shown below the main actions. It lists your number cards, your current round total, and any bonuses. Other players' areas are also shown so you can judge how risky the round is.

The round ends when all players have stopped or busted, the deck runs out, or someone completes Flip 7.

**Cards**

* **Number cards:** Add their value to your area. If you draw a number you already have, you bust unless a Second Chance saves you.
* **Modifier cards:** Add bonus points, such as +2 or +10.
* **Double:** Doubles the value of your number cards before modifiers are added.
* **Second Chance:** Protects you once from drawing a repeated number. If you already have a Second Chance, you must give the extra one to another eligible player.
* **Stop:** Choose a player who is still playing this round. That player stops and banks their current round total. If you are the only player still playing, you stop yourself automatically.
* **Flip Three:** Choose a player who must flip three cards in a row.

**Scoring**

Your round total is the sum of your number cards. If you have Double, that number total is doubled. Modifier cards are added after doubling.

If you collect seven different number cards, you score the Flip 7 bonus of 15 points and the round ends immediately.

Busted players score 0 for the round. Players who stopped or survived until the round ends bank their current round total.

**Options**

* **Target Score:** The total score needed to win after a completed round (default 200, range 50 to 1000).

**Information Actions**

* **Check my area:** Speaks your current cards, bonuses, and round total.
* **Check the table:** Speaks the round, target score, and each player's round status and total score.
* **Check the deck:** Speaks how many cards are in the deck and discard pile.
* **Check scores:** Speaks the accumulated match scores.

**Keyboard Shortcuts**

* **Space:** Draw a card.
* **H:** Stop and bank.
* **C:** Check your area.
* **Shift+C:** Check the table.
* **D:** Check the deck.
* **1 through 9:** Read that numbered card in your area.
* **0:** Read the tenth card in your area, if available.
* **S:** Check scores.
* **Shift+S:** Open detailed scores.
* **T:** Check whose turn it is.
