**Table Tennis**

Table Tennis is a real-time spatial-audio game with official singles for two players and doubles for two teams of two. Humans and bots can occupy any seat.

**Gameplay**

* Listen to the real table bounce to locate the ball in stereo.
* For every ball, silently choose the direction where the bounce came from: Left for left, Up for center, or Right for right.
* Before serving, choose the destination with Left, Up for center, or Right, then press C. During a rally, use the arrow matching where the ball came from and press X; immediately after contact, press Left, Up, or Right within the short window to choose the return destination.

The paddle moves across the court instead of teleporting. Incoming position, real paddle reach, return destination, and timing all matter. A slightly early or late contact is less accurate; a large error hits the net, goes out, or misses. After X, a second short window selects the destination; if no direction is chosen, the ball goes through the center. Up means center. There is no artificial flight or movement sound.

**Rules and Scoring**

Each game is played to 11 points and must be won by two points. Service changes after every two points, then after every point at 10–10. C performs a legal service automatically; return faults come from the player's reach and timing, not from a hidden random miss. Choose one game or a best-of-three, five, or seven match when creating the table.

In doubles, teams have two players, service is diagonal, partners alternate every return, and the official server and receiver sequence is preserved. Sides change after each game and at five points in the deciding game.

**Game Options**

* **Match length:** One game or best of three, five, or seven.
* **Mode:** Official singles or official doubles.
* **Ball pace:** Relaxed, standard, or fast.
* **Bot difficulty:** Easy, normal, or hard.
* **Table language:** English, Spanish, Portuguese, Vietnamese, or Persian. A new table starts in its creator's language; unsupported languages use English.

**Audio**

Paddle, table, and net impacts use real CC0 table-tennis recordings, trimmed and lightly balanced for play. The table bounce is positioned in stereo at runtime so you can locate the ball without a duplicated direction cue.

**Keyboard Shortcuts**

* **Left Arrow:** Move the paddle left or choose the left side as the destination.
* **Up Arrow:** Move to center or choose center as the destination.
* **Right Arrow:** Move right or choose the right side as the destination.
* **C:** Serve.
* **X:** Return the ball.
* **P:** Read your position and the ball status.
* **M:** Read the match status and score.
