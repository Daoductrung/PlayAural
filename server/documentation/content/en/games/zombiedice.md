\*\*Zombie Dice\*\*

\*\*What Is Zombie Dice?\*\*

Designed by Steve Jackson and first published by Steve Jackson Games in 2010, Zombie Dice is a fast push-your-luck dice game for 2 to 8 players. In a push-your-luck game, every success makes continuing more tempting, but one bad roll can wipe out everything not yet secured during that turn.

You are a hungry zombie chasing victims for their brains. Brains score points, footprints let victims escape for the moment, and shotgun blasts bring the hunt to a bloody end. The longer you keep rolling, the more brains you might collect—and the more you risk losing.

\*\*How To Win\*\*

Bank more brains than anyone else. The official winning target is 13 brains. Reaching the target does not guarantee victory: the current round must be completed, and the player with the highest score at its end wins.

\*\*The Thirteen Dice\*\*

The cup holds thirteen six-sided dice. Their colors indicate how dangerous they are:

\* \*\*Six green dice:\*\* three brains, two footprints, and one shotgun on each die. Green is the safest color.
\* \*\*Four yellow dice:\*\* two brains, two footprints, and two shotguns on each die.
\* \*\*Three red dice:\*\* one brain, two footprints, and three shotguns on each die. Red is the most dangerous color.

You do not know a die's color while it remains in the cup. Once rolled, its color and face are public.

\*\*The Three Faces\*\*

\* \*\*Brain:\*\* Set the die aside. It adds one unbanked brain to the current turn.
\* \*\*Footprint:\*\* Keep the die ready. If you roll again, every footprint die must be rerolled.
\* \*\*Shotgun:\*\* Set the die aside. Shotguns accumulate throughout the turn.

Unbanked brains become permanent points only when you stop safely.

\*\*Starting The Game\*\*

The first player is chosen randomly, and the complete turn order is announced. That order remains fixed throughout the main game.

Every turn begins with all thirteen dice in the cup. Nothing rolled during the previous turn carries over.

\*\*Your Turn\*\*

Choose \*\*Roll 3 dice\*\*. Three hidden dice are drawn from the cup and rolled, and the color and face of each die are announced.

If you have fewer than three shotguns after the roll, choose whether to stop or roll again:

\* \*\*Stop and score:\*\* Bank every brain collected during this turn and pass play to the next player.
\* \*\*Roll again:\*\* Reroll every footprint die, then draw enough hidden dice to bring the new roll back to three dice.

New dice are not drawn until after you choose to continue, so their colors are unknown when you commit to the roll. After at least one safe roll, stopping with zero brains is legal.

\*\*Getting Shotgunned\*\*

As soon as your turn reaches three or more shotguns, you are shotgunned. Your turn ends immediately, and you lose every unbanked brain from that turn, including any brains rolled alongside the final shotgun. Brains banked during earlier turns are safe.

All thirteen dice return to the cup for the next player.

\*\*When The Cup Runs Low\*\*

Before every roll after the first, the number of dice in the cup is checked. If fewer than three remain, all brain dice set aside during the turn return to the cup. Your unbanked brain total remains unchanged, and shotgun dice stay outside the cup.

The cup is refilled whenever it contains fewer than three dice, even if footprint dice mean you need to draw only one or two. Every footprint die is still rerolled, and only enough new dice are drawn to make a roll of three.

For example, suppose you have two footprints to reroll and two dice remain in the cup. The brain dice are returned to the cup first. You keep credit for the brains already collected, even if one of those dice is drawn and rolled again.

\*\*The Final Round\*\*

When a player banks enough brains to reach the target, everyone later in the turn order receives one final turn. Players earlier in the order have already played the same number of turns and do not play again.

A later player may overtake the person who reached the target. When the round ends, the player with the highest score wins. If the player who reaches the target is last in the order, the result is resolved immediately because everyone has already taken an equal number of turns.

\*\*Tiebreaks\*\*

If two or more players share the highest score at the end of the final round, only those leaders enter a tiebreak. Each receives one complete turn in the original order, and any brains they bank are added to their existing score.

If the leaders remain tied, they play another tiebreak round. This continues until one player leads alone.

\*\*Keeping Track Of The Game\*\*

\* \*\*Check turn totals:\*\* Hear the current player's unbanked brains, accumulated shotguns, and footprints.
\* \*\*Review current turn:\*\* Review the current player, banked score, turn totals, number of dice in the cup, footprint colors, set-aside brain and shotgun dice, and latest roll.
\* \*\*Review table:\*\* Review the target, current phase, turn order, active turn, and every player's score.
\* \*\*Check scores:\*\* Hear the current standings.
\* \*\*Whose turn and who is at the table:\*\* Hear the active player or the table roster.

\*\*Spatial Audio\*\*

With headphones, rolls, bites, growls, and other table sounds come from the acting player's seat. When a player is shotgunned, gunfire starts at the center of the table and travels toward that player's seat. Beyond the table, wandering zombies move through the surrounding swamp.

\*\*Table Option\*\*

\* \*\*Brains needed to win:\*\* Choose a target from 5 to 50. The default and official target is 13.

\*\*Keyboard Shortcuts\*\*

\* \*\*R:\*\* Roll three dice or roll again.
\* \*\*B:\*\* Stop and bank the brains from this turn.
\* \*\*C:\*\* Check the current turn totals.
\* \*\*V:\*\* Review the current turn in detail.
\* \*\*Shift+V:\*\* Review the table.
\* \*\*S:\*\* Check scores.
\* \*\*T:\*\* Check whose turn it is.
\* \*\*Ctrl+U:\*\* Hear who is at the table.
