\*\*Welcome to PlayAural\*\*

PlayAural is an online gaming platform designed around sound, speech, and accessible controls. Menus, game events, chat, status updates, and supported voice features are presented in a way that can be followed without relying on visual information.

\*\*Where You Can Play\*\*

\* \*\*Desktop client:\*\* Best for users who want a dedicated Windows app and full keyboard control.
\* \*\*Web client:\*\* Designed for browser play and works especially well on phones and tablets through a mobile browser.
\* \*\*Mobile client:\*\* A dedicated mobile app built for gesture-based play with built-in speech.

\*\*How to Start Playing\*\*

1\. \*\*Open Play:\*\* Choose Play from the main menu to browse the game list.
2\. \*\*Check Active Tables:\*\* Use Active Tables to find tables that are already open and ready to join.
3\. \*\*Open Friends:\*\* Use Friends to manage your friends list, send friend requests, and send private messages.
4\. \*\*Open Options:\*\* Use Options to change language, speech, sound, and other personal settings.
5\. \*\*Join or create a table:\*\* Once you are at a table, you can invite friends, add computer-controlled players, or wait for others to join.

\*\*What You Can Do at a Table\*\*

When you enter a table, you can usually:

\* Start the game if you are the host.
\* Review the game rules through How to Play.
\* Check Game Info to hear the current table settings.
\* Use Who's at the table to hear who is present.
\* Chat with other people at the table or send private messages.
\* Join the table's voice chat on the PlayAural clients that support voice playback and microphone access.
\* If you are the host, open Host Management and choose Switch to Another Game to move everyone who is still present into a fresh waiting lobby for another game. Playing and spectating roles, bots, table ownership, privacy, bans, and voice chat stay with the table; the old match, options, teams, ready state, and pending invitations are discarded after you confirm.
\* If you are the host during a game, open Host Management to substitute a spectator into an active player seat. A human currently in that seat must agree first. The substitute receives the seat's exact game state, remaining turn time, and eventual result attribution. If you give up your own seat, you become a spectator but remain the table host.

\*\*Chat and Social Features\*\*

PlayAural is built to be social as well as accessible.

\* \*\*Friends:\*\* Keep track of the people you enjoy playing with.
\* \*\*Private messages:\*\* Send direct messages without leaving your current table. Messages you send and receive are kept in the Private Messages history buffer for the current session.
\* \*\*Online users:\*\* Check who is currently online.
\* \*\*Global chat:\*\* Outside a table, ordinary messages go to your selected global-chat language channel. At a table, type `/g` followed by your message to send it to that channel; ordinary messages stay at the table.

\*\*Learning the Controls\*\*

If you are completely new, read Global Controls next. That guide explains the desktop keyboard shortcuts and the mobile gestures in beginner-friendly language.

\*\*Community\*\*

PlayAural aims to be a friendly place for new and experienced users alike. Before your first serious session, take a moment to read the Community Rules so you know what is expected at the table and in chat.
