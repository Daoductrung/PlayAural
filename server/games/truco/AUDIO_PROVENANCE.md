# Truco audio provenance

Per-asset record for the three music tracks in `game_truco/` (identical copies in
`client/sounds/`, `web_client/sounds/` and `mobile_client/sounds/`). Every other
Truco sound effect reuses an existing PlayAural asset (see `audio.py`) and adds no
new file. Source pages and license statements were read on 22 October 2026.

Derivation for every file: the downloaded MP3 (256 kbps) was converted, without
editing or trimming, to stereo 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis (`libvorbis`, quality 4).

## Terms to be aware of

- The files are used under the Pixabay Content License (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/),
  not CC0. Attribution is not required, and modification is allowed. The license
  forbids selling or distributing the content on a standalone basis, so the tracks
  are shipped only as integrated game music and must not be offered as separate
  downloads. Keep this record with the public repository and recheck the current
  terms before redistributing or replacing them.
- A Pixabay page shows the license currently offered for the file. Pixabay's summary
  does not address bundling in open-source software, and only the full Terms of
  Service are binding.
- All three tracks are labeled AI generated by Pixabay. The Bingo music is used
  under the same license, as recorded in `server/games/bingo/AUDIO_PROVENANCE.md`.
- The sound-pack version was not changed; bumping it is a maintainer release action.

## Replacing these tracks

The music may be replaced freely: change the constants in
`server/games/truco/audio.py`, update the three client copies and this record, and
regenerate the Web and Mobile sound manifests.
